# Inaya Business Workspace is now available as a desktop app

Your Business Workspace — departments, projects, documents, approvals, AI Assistant — now runs as a real app on your computer, not just in a browser tab.

## What you get

- **A real installed app** with its own icon, Start Menu entry, and taskbar presence — just like any other program on your computer.
- **Runs in the background.** Closing the window doesn't quit it — it keeps running quietly in your system tray so you don't miss anything. Click the tray icon to bring it back.
- **Desktop notifications.** If you're an owner or admin, you'll get a native Windows notification when a document is waiting on your approval — no need to keep the app open and watch for it.
- **Automatic updates.** When a new version is released, the app will ask if you'd like to install it — one click and you're up to date, no need to manually download anything again.
- **Sign in your way.** Both "Continue with Google" and email sign-in links work exactly as they do on the web.

Everything else — your documents, encryption, permissions, sharing, the AI Assistant — works exactly the same as it does in your browser today. This is the same Business Workspace, just in its own window.

## Available for

- **Windows** — ready now, fully tested. You may see a Windows SmartScreen warning ("Windows protected your PC") when installing — this is expected for now, not a sign of a problem. It shows up because the installer isn't digitally signed with a paid certificate yet; we're deferring that cost until mainnet. Click "More info" → "Run anyway" to proceed.
- **Linux** — available now. A real user tested it and reported a visual glitch where dropdown menus (like the document-visibility selector) showed a bright orange glow that looked like an error — that's now fixed. Still hasn't had a full pass on real Linux hardware beyond that, so please keep the reports coming.

(macOS isn't available yet.)

## We're on testnet — please report anything odd

This is an early release. If something looks wrong, doesn't work, or behaves differently than the website, please tell us — that's exactly the kind of feedback that helps us fix it before a wider release. The Linux dropdown glitch above is a good example: a real report led straight to a real fix.
