## INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

**This Agreement** is made on **[DATE]**

**BETWEEN:**

**[INAYA LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, a company/entity organized under the laws of Pakistan, having its registered address at **[REGISTERED ADDRESS]** ("the **Company**")

**AND:**

**[FOUNDER FULL LEGAL NAME]**, holding CNIC/passport number **[ID NUMBER]**, residing at **[ADDRESS]** ("the **Founder**")

(each a "**Party**", together the "**Parties**")

### RECITALS

A. The Founder has been involved in conceiving, designing, developing, and/or contributing to the technology, software, documentation, brand, and other work product known as "Inaya" / "Inaya Network" (the "**Project**"), including before the Company's formal registration.

B. The Parties wish to record, with legal certainty, that all intellectual property created by the Founder for or in connection with the Project belongs to the Company, regardless of when it was created relative to the Company's formal incorporation.

C. The Founder enters into this Agreement voluntarily and confirms having had the opportunity to seek independent legal advice.

### 1. DEFINITIONS

**"Company IP"** means all Intellectual Property Rights in or relating to the Project, including but not limited to: source code (in any repository, including the ones listed in the Company's IP Asset Register), smart contracts and protocol software, the Business Workspace, AI and retrieval/RAG systems, the Security Layer, node software, mobile and desktop applications, SDKs, documentation, the whitepaper, the website, branding (including the "INAYA" and "INAYA NETWORK" marks and associated logos), and any other original work created for or in connection with the Project.

**"Intellectual Property Rights"** means all copyright, database rights, design rights, patent rights, trademark rights, trade secrets, and any other intellectual or industrial property rights, whether registered or unregistered, anywhere in the world.

**"Pre-Existing IP"** means any Intellectual Property Rights owned by the Founder before starting work on the Project, and not created for or in connection with it, listed in **Schedule A**.

### 2. ASSIGNMENT

2.1 The Founder hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company, with full title guarantee, all right, title, and interest — present and future, in Pakistan and worldwide — in and to all Company IP created, conceived, or contributed to by the Founder, whether alone or with others, in connection with the Project.

2.2 This assignment applies regardless of whether the work was created before or after the date of this Agreement, and regardless of whether it was created using the Company's resources, provided it was created for or in connection with the Project.

2.3 This Agreement does not assign any Pre-Existing IP listed in Schedule A. The Founder grants the Company a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to use any Pre-Existing IP to the extent it is incorporated into the Project.

### 3. MORAL RIGHTS

3.1 To the extent permitted under the Copyright Ordinance, 1962 (Pakistan) and any applicable law, the Founder waives all moral rights in the Company IP, including the right to be identified as the author and the right to object to derogatory treatment of the work.

### 4. FURTHER ASSURANCES

4.1 The Founder agrees to execute any further documents and take any further action reasonably requested by the Company — including for copyright, trademark, or patent registration purposes — to give full effect to this Agreement, both during and after their involvement with the Project.

### 5. WARRANTIES

5.1 The Founder warrants that:
(a) the Company IP assigned under this Agreement is the Founder's own original work, or the Founder otherwise has the right to assign it;
(b) to the Founder's knowledge, the Company IP does not infringe the Intellectual Property Rights of any third party;
(c) the Founder has not granted, and will not grant, any conflicting rights in the Company IP to any third party.

### 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 The Founder agrees to keep confidential all non-public technical and business information relating to the Project, both during and after their involvement, except as required by law or with the Company's prior written consent. [Cross-reference: see the Confidentiality & Trade Secret Framework for the Company's classification scheme.]

### 7. GOVERNING LAW

7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Pakistan. The courts of **[CITY]**, Pakistan shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

### 8. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

8.1 This Agreement, together with its Schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties on this subject and supersedes any prior understanding.

**Drafting notes for whoever finalizes this (delete before use):**
- Confirm the exact legal name and registration number of the Inaya entity once incorporated/confirmed — every founder's copy must reference the same entity.
- Pakistan's copyright framework (Copyright Ordinance, 1962, as amended) does recognize moral rights; Clause 3 should be reviewed by counsel for enforceability of the waiver, since some jurisdictions limit how far moral rights can be waived.
- If any founder contributed to the Project as an employee of a *different* company, or under a university/employer IP policy, that must be flagged and resolved before signing — Schedule A exists for exactly this.
- Consider whether founders based outside Pakistan need a jurisdiction-specific rider (this template assumes Pakistani law governs regardless of founder location, which should be confirmed as enforceable for each founder's actual residence).
