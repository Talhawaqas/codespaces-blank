Inaya Starter Reports for Microsoft Power BI

1. Install the Inaya connector first (Inaya.mez, see Admin > Integrations > Power BI in Inaya).
2. Open 'Inaya Starter Reports.pbip' in Power BI Desktop.
3. Refresh, and sign in to Inaya when asked. Pick your company and allow.
4. Change RangeStart / RangeEnd in Transform data > Manage parameters to load more or less history.

Amounts are in each record's own currency: use the Currency slicer. Datasets your Inaya role cannot read load empty.
Full guide: https://www.inayanetwork.com/docs/products/power-bi
