# Inaya Business OS User Guide

Inaya Business OS (formerly Inaya Business Workspace) is a business operations platform: run your whole business on one connected system. This guide is also available as a PDF with pictures: /docs/inaya-business-os-user-guide.pdf

Part 1, Getting Started: Create your company, choose a package and find your way around.

## 1. Getting Started (Part 1: Getting Started)

For: Everyone

Inaya Business OS is one system for your whole business: the office (accounts, staff, stock and buying), the shop counter (point of sale) and online selling. You use it in your web browser, in the desktop app for Windows or Linux, or on Android. You do not need any special computer skills: if you can use email and a web browser, you can use Inaya.

**Create your company**

1. Go to inayanetwork.com/business.
2. Press "Create a company".
3. Type your company name and choose your type of business (for example general business, education or hospitality). This decides which industry screens you see. You can change it later.
4. Type your work email and press "Create company", or press "Continue with Google".
5. Open the email we send you and press the sign-in link. If it does not arrive within a few minutes, look in your spam or junk folder.

**Choose your package**

1. A new company first sees the package screen. Press "Request activation" on the package you want.
2. Give us your name, phone or WhatsApp number, and how you will pay (NayaPay or bank transfer), then press "Send request".
3. Our team contacts you within one working day and emails an invoice.
4. Pay using the invoice number as the reference. We switch your package on as soon as the payment arrives and email you.

**Sign in next time**

1. Go to inayanetwork.com/business and press "Sign in".
2. Email link: type your email and press "Send sign-in link", then open the email. Or press "Continue with Google".
3. Passkey (fastest): press "Sign in with a passkey" and confirm with your fingerprint, face or phone PIN. Add a passkey first under Account Security (see IT and Security).
4. Company sign-in (SSO, Enterprise): type your work email and press "Sign in with company SSO". You sign in on your company's Microsoft, Google or Okta page and come straight back.

> On Professional you can add single Business features as add-ons: appointments, email campaigns, Inaya Connect (WhatsApp), a client portal, a custom domain, API access or extra storage. Choose them on the request form or later in Billing. Prices are in the Billing section of this guide.

> In Pakistan, packages are invoiced in PKR with a one-time setup fee (PKR 10,000 for Starter, PKR 25,000 for the others). Yearly billing costs 10 months instead of 12.

> Inaya Commerce (the shop counter and online selling) is an extra: Commerce Starter on Professional ($49 / PKR 8,000 a month), the Commerce add-on on Business ($79 / PKR 12,000), and it is included in Enterprise.

## 2. Finding Your Way Around (Part 1: Getting Started)

For: Everyone

The top row of big buttons takes you to each part of the business: Home, Sales, Purchasing, Inventory, Accounting, People, Documents, Workplace, Reports and Admin. When you open one, its menu appears on the left. The path at the top (for example Admin > Storage and IT > Cloud Backup) always tells you where you are.

**Everyday controls**

1. Search (top right, or press Ctrl + K): type the name of any screen, customer or product and press Enter.
2. The bell shows your notifications, for example something waiting for your approval.
3. Your name (top right) opens your account, the look of the screens (Light, Dark, Neon or Calm) and Sign out.
4. The round robot button (bottom right) opens the AI assistant. Ask it questions in normal words.

**Find any module**

1. Open Home, then "All apps" on the left.
2. Type what you are looking for, or browse the tiles.
3. Press a tile to open it. Pin the ones you use most so they are always one click away.

## 3. Sales and Customers (Part 2: Back Office)

For: Sales staff, managers

Sales is where you keep your customers, deals and sales orders, and where you find the shop counter, customer growth tools and your online store.

**Add a customer**

1. Open Sales, then "Customers and deals".
2. Press "New contact".
3. Type the name, phone and email, then save.

**Customer growth: loyalty, gift cards and offers**

1. Open Sales > Customer growth.
2. Turn on loyalty points, create gift cards, or set up an offer (for example 10% off a category for a week).
3. Points, gift cards and offers are used automatically at the shop counter.

## 4. Inventory and Purchasing (Part 2: Back Office)

For: Store keepers, buyers, managers

Inventory keeps every product and how many you have in each branch or warehouse. Purchasing is for buying from suppliers.

**Add a product**

1. Open Inventory > Products and press "New product".
2. Type the name, SKU (your own short code), barcode, price and tax category, then save.
3. Record the stock you already have: Inventory > Stock control > Counts, or receive it on a purchase order.

**Move and count stock**

1. Transfers: move stock from one branch to another. It leaves one branch and arrives at the other only when received.
2. Counts: count the shelf, type what you found, and approve the difference.

**Buy from a supplier**

1. Open Purchasing > Buying > Purchase requests and raise a request.
2. When it is approved, turn it into a purchase order and send it to the supplier.
3. When the goods arrive, receive them on the order. Stock goes up automatically.

## 5. Accounting (Part 2: Back Office)

For: Accountants, owners

Accounting holds your invoices, expenses, payments and books. Sales from the shop counter and online store are posted to the books automatically.

**Send an invoice**

1. Open Accounting > Books > Invoices and press "New invoice".
2. Choose the customer, add the items and the due date, then save and send.
3. When the customer pays, record the payment. The invoice shows as paid.

**Record an expense**

1. Open Accounting > Expenses and press "New expense".
2. Type the amount, category and date, and attach the receipt photo or PDF.

**See how the business is doing**

1. Open Accounting > Reports for profit and loss, what customers owe you and what you owe suppliers.

## 6. People (Part 2: Back Office)

For: HR, managers

People keeps your staff records, leave, team structure, rotas and commissions.

**Add a staff member**

1. Open People > Staff > Employees and press "New employee".
2. Type their details and job, and attach documents such as their contract.

**Leave**

1. Staff ask for leave from People > Leave.
2. Their manager approves or rejects it. Everyone can see who is away.

**Time, rota and commissions**

1. Open People > Time, rota and commissions.
2. Plan the week's shifts per branch and publish them. Staff clock in and out at their branch.
3. Set a commission rule (for example 2% of sales) and Inaya works it out from the till.

## 7. Documents (Part 2: Back Office)

For: Everyone

Keep company documents in one safe place, organised by department and project. Files are encrypted on your own computer before they are uploaded, so nobody at Inaya can read them.

**Upload a document**

1. Open Documents, choose the department and project, and press upload.
2. Choose who can see it: only you and people you add, the department, or the project team.
3. Set a passkey (the password that encrypts this file; it is not the same as a sign-in passkey). Keep it safe: it cannot be recovered, by anyone.

**Approve and share**

1. Send a document for review. The approver approves or rejects it under Home > Approvals.
2. To share outside the company, create a secure link with an end date. You can cancel it at any time.

## 8. Workplace: Tasks, Chat and Help (Part 2: Back Office)

For: Everyone

Workplace is for day-to-day teamwork: tasks, projects, team chat and video meetings, automations, the AI assistant, help, and sending us your ideas.

**Give someone a task**

1. Open Workplace > Tasks and press "New task".
2. Type what needs doing, choose the person and the due date, then save. They get a notification.

**Suggest an improvement**

1. Open Workplace > Automation and help > Suggest an improvement.
2. Describe what you need and how important it is, then send. Others can vote for the same idea.
3. We plan ideas into our work and tell you when yours is planned and when it is ready.

## 9. Inaya Connect: WhatsApp for Your Business (Part 2: Back Office)

For: Owners, managers, support and sales staff

Inaya Connect links your company's own WhatsApp Business number to Inaya. Staff ask questions on WhatsApp and get answers from your own data, bills sent on WhatsApp go to AI Bookkeeper, and every customer chat lands in one team inbox. Included in Business and Enterprise, and available as an add-on for Professional. Meta bills WhatsApp messages to your own WhatsApp Business Account.

**Connect your WhatsApp number (about 20 to 30 minutes)**

1. Open Sales > Customer service > Inaya Connect (an owner or admin), then the Connection tab, and follow "Connect your WhatsApp Business number".
2. The steps on screen walk you through Meta: create a Meta app for WhatsApp, copy your phone number ID and WhatsApp Business Account ID, an access token and the app secret, and paste them into Inaya.
3. Point Meta's webhook to Inaya using the address and verify token Inaya shows, then send "Hello" to your number from your phone: it appears in the Inbox.
4. Inaya never asks for your Facebook password or admin access to your Meta account.

**Let staff ask questions on WhatsApp**

1. Each staff member opens Sales > Customer service > Inaya Connect > My WhatsApp and presses "Get my link code".
2. They tap "Open WhatsApp" and press Send, so the code goes from their own WhatsApp to your business number (or they scan the QR code). Their phone is now linked; "Unlink my phone" removes it.
3. They can ask in plain words, for example "How much did we sell today?" or "Stock of item X at my branch". Answers follow each person's permissions. Approvals, payments and price or stock changes stay inside Inaya.

**Customer chats and documents**

1. Every customer message appears in the Inbox. Reply, assign it, add a note, close it, or turn it into a Support Desk ticket.
2. A photo of a bill, invoice or receipt goes to AI Bookkeeper as a draft for review; nothing is paid automatically. Other files are saved in the dated "Inaya Connect" folder and the sender is emailed where it is.
3. The customer assistant is off by default. When you turn it on, it answers from your published help articles and shares a customer's own orders, repairs, appointments and invoices only after a 6-digit code sent to their email.

> Customers can send STOP to stop messages and START to receive them again.

> Full guide: inayanetwork.com/docs/products/inaya-connect

## 11. Commerce: the Shop Counter (Front Office) (Part 3: Front Office)

For: Cashiers, supervisors, shop managers

Point of Sale is your till. It works on a normal computer, a laptop, a tablet or a phone. Every sale updates your stock, your books and the cash in the drawer automatically. Find it under Sales > Shop counter > Point of Sale.

**Start your day: open a shift**

1. Open Point of Sale. At the top, choose your till (register).
2. Press "Open shift" and type the cash already in the drawer (the float).
3. You are ready to sell.

**Make a sale**

1. Scan the barcode, type the product name, or tap the product.
2. To sell more than one, press + or type the number.
3. Press "Pay" (or F9). Choose Cash, Bank transfer or another method and type the amount. The till shows the change.
4. Press complete. The receipt appears: print it, email it or save it.

**Returns, discounts and approvals**

1. Returns: open the Sales tab, find the sale (or type its receipt number), press Return and choose the items.
2. Discounts above your limit need a supervisor or manager to approve them from their own screen (Approvals tab). This stops mistakes and misuse.

**End your day: close the shift**

1. Count the cash in the drawer.
2. Press "Close shift" and type the amount you counted.
3. Inaya shows if the drawer is over or short. Big differences are flagged for the manager.

> Keyboard shortcuts: F2 search, F4 customer, F8 park a sale for later, F9 pay, Esc close a window.

## 12. Commerce: Printing, Cash Drawer, Scanning and Labels (Part 3: Front Office)

For: Shop managers, cashiers

These till extras are in Beta: they are being confirmed with our first pilot shops. Silent printing and opening the cash drawer need the Inaya desktop app (Windows or Linux). Settings are saved on each till computer.

**Receipt printer and cash drawer (desktop app, Beta)**

1. Open Point of Sale > Setup (managers).
2. Under "This till's receipt printer and cash drawer", choose your printer: one on this computer (USB), a network printer (type its IP address) or a laser / A4 printer.
3. Choose the paper: 80 mm or 58 mm.
4. Choose how the cash drawer opens: through the receipt printer, a USB drawer or trigger box, or a network drawer controller.
5. Press "Print a test receipt" and "Test the cash drawer" to check.

**Laser or inkjet printer: full A4 or A5 receipt (Beta)**

1. In the same box, choose "Receipts on a laser / inkjet printer": Full A4 page or Full A5 page.
2. Leave it on "Till-roll strip" if you print to a thermal receipt printer.

**Scan with a camera or your phone (Beta)**

1. Camera: press "Camera" next to the search box and point the camera at the barcode.
2. Phone as a scanner for a computer till: press "Phone". A QR code appears.
3. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera and open the link (no sign-in needed on the phone). Press "Start scanning".
4. Every barcode the phone reads appears at the till within a second. The button shows "Phone ✓" while connected. Press "Disconnect the phone" when you finish.

**Print barcode labels (Beta)**

1. Open Point of Sale > Labels.
2. Search or scan each product and type how many labels you need.
3. Choose the label size (for example 50 x 25 mm, or an A4 sticker sheet). Tick product name and price if you want them.
4. Press "Print labels" and choose your label printer. In the desktop app you can also send labels straight to a Zebra or TSC / Xprinter printer.

> Inaya does not supply hardware. You buy your own printer, cash drawer and scanner; see our Compatible Hardware guide for what works.

## 13. Commerce: Selling When the Internet Drops (Beta) (Part 3: Front Office)

For: Shop managers, cashiers

If the internet goes down, your till can keep selling cash sales. The sales are saved safely on the till and sent to Inaya automatically when the connection comes back.

**Switch it on (manager, once)**

1. Open Point of Sale > Setup.
2. Under "Offline selling", tick "Let tills keep selling when the internet drops".
3. Set the limits if you want (how many hours, how many sales, largest sale). Press "Save settings".
4. Each till gets ready by itself while it is online with a shift open.

**When the internet drops**

1. The till shows "Offline" at the top. Keep selling: cash only, at shelf prices.
2. Receipts still print and the drawer still opens. Offline receipt numbers start with OFF-.
3. If the computer or app restarts with no internet, open the desktop app and press "Open the till offline".

**When the internet comes back**

1. The till sends the offline sales automatically. You do not need to do anything.
2. Inaya checks every sale again before it goes into the books, stock and shift cash.
3. Anything that does not match goes to the manager under "Offline review", never straight into the books.

> Offline, discounts, customers, gift cards, loyalty and card payments are not available. Wait for the connection to come back for those.

> Do not close the shift while offline sales are still waiting: the till will remind you.

## 14. Commerce: Online Selling, Shopify and WooCommerce (Part 4: Online Selling: WooCommerce and Shopify)

For: Shop owners, online sales staff

Sell online from the same products, stock and books as your shop. You can have your own Inaya web store, and connect a Shopify or WooCommerce store you already have. Find it under Sales > Online store & Shopify / WooCommerce.

**Your own Inaya web store**

1. Open the Store tab. Press "Create store" and choose your store's web address.
2. Add your logo, colours and home page text, then press "Save draft".
3. Open the Products tab and choose which products to show online.
4. Set up Delivery and Payments (bank transfer, cash on delivery, or cards through your own Stripe or Safepay account).
5. Press "Publish store". Online orders arrive under Orders and take stock from the same shelves.

**Connect WooCommerce**

1. In WordPress, open WooCommerce > Settings > Advanced > REST API and create a key with Read/Write access.
2. In Inaya, open the "Shopify & WooCommerce" tab and press "Connect WooCommerce".
3. Type your shop address, then paste the Consumer key (ck_...) and Consumer secret (cs_...). Press "Save keys".
4. Press "Import & match products". Inaya matches your WooCommerce products to your Inaya products.
5. From now on, stock updates go to WooCommerce and new WooCommerce orders come into Inaya once, with their stock taken off.

**Connect Shopify**

1. Open the "Shopify & WooCommerce" tab and press "Connect Shopify".
2. Type your Shopify address (yourshop.myshopify.com) and press "Install Inaya on Shopify", then approve on the Shopify screen.
3. Press "Import & match products", then use "Send stock now" and "Get orders now" whenever you want an immediate update.

> One-click Shopify installation is being reviewed by Shopify for all shops. If the install does not work for your shop yet, contact us and we will connect it with you.

> Your plan decides how many stores you can connect: Commerce Starter one (Shopify or WooCommerce), the Business add-on one of each, Enterprise three.

> Your plan also sets how many Shopify and WooCommerce orders come in each month: 300 on Commerce Starter, 1,000 on the Business add-on, unlimited on Enterprise. Need more? Extra 1,000 orders cost $15 (PKR 2,500), every month or for one month only, up to three blocks. Orders over the limit wait and come in by themselves once there is room.

## 15. IT and Security (Part 5: IT Services)

For: IT staff, owners and admins

Admin holds the IT and security tools. Most small businesses only need a few of them; your IT person or Inaya can help set up the rest. Find them under Admin.

**Passkeys: sign in with your fingerprint or face (every package)**

1. Open Account Security: your name (top right) > Account security, or Admin > My account > Account Security.
2. Type a name such as "Work laptop" and press "Add a passkey". Confirm with your fingerprint, face or device PIN.
3. Next time, press "Sign in with a passkey" on the sign-in screen. It counts as two-step verification and cannot be phished. You can add up to 10 and remove any of them.

**Company single sign-on (SSO, Enterprise)**

1. In your identity provider (Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, Okta or any OpenID Connect provider), create a web application and register the redirect address Inaya shows under Admin > Company setup > Settings > Single sign-on.
2. In Inaya, enter the issuer address, client ID, client secret and your staff email domains, then press "Save" and "Test the provider".
3. Tick "SSO is on" and save. Staff then choose "Sign in with company SSO". They still need to be invited to the company first.
4. Optional: "Require SSO" turns off email links and Google sign-in for those email domains.

**Admin Dashboard**

1. Open Admin > Security > Admin Dashboard.
2. Read the tiles at the top (people signed in, devices, anything risky).
3. Use the "Turn on more protection" list to switch on extra protection with one click each.

**Identity & Access (staff joining and leaving)**

1. Open Admin > Security > Identity & Access.
2. Connect your staff directory (for example Microsoft Entra ID or your HR system).
3. When someone joins, changes job or leaves, their Inaya access changes automatically. A leaver is cut off at once.

**Ransomware protection and recovery**

1. Open Admin > Security > Ransomware Signals to switch on warnings about suspicious activity.
2. Open Security & Resilience Controls and Resilience Testing to see your protection and run recovery tests.
3. If ransomware ever strikes, protected copies that an attacker cannot change let you roll back to a clean point in time. Recovery is tested, not just promised.

**Cloud Backup (copy your other cloud storage into Inaya)**

1. Open Admin > Storage and IT > Cloud Backup.
2. Under "Add a credential", choose your provider (AWS, Azure or Google Cloud), type the access details and press "Save Credential".
3. Create a backup schedule. Only changed files are copied each time, and every run is checked.

**Sovereign NAS (your own network storage in the office)**

1. Open Admin > Storage and IT > Sovereign NAS.
2. Type a name and the appliance's address (IP), then press "Register & check health".
3. Create shared folders for staff, with snapshots, backup and protection against changes.

**S3-compatible storage and databases**

1. S3-Compatible Storage: press "+ New S3 credential" to use Inaya storage from tools such as the AWS command line or rclone.
2. Databases: create a managed database for your own systems, where your Inaya setup includes it.

> Some tools belong to particular packages: the Admin Dashboard, organization-wide policies, audit logs, device and session management, client portals, custom domains and API keys are in Business and Enterprise; SSO, custom retention, compliance readiness and customer-managed keys are in Enterprise. If your package does not include a tool, Inaya tells you which package does.

> Integrations, Webhooks and API Keys (under Admin) connect Inaya to your other software. Only give API keys to people you trust, and cancel them when no longer needed.

**Billing**

2. Owners and admins can download all the company's data as a file from Billing at any time.
