## What it is

Education OS is the industry edition of the Business Workspace for small and mid-sized schools, colleges and academies in Pakistan, India, Bangladesh and the UAE. Choose **Education** as the industry when you create the company (or later in Settings). It runs in the ERP-style layout next to Finance, HR and Documents.

## Roles

Owners and admins give each staff member an Education role in **School setup → Staff roles**:

| Role | Sees and does |
| --- | --- |
| Principal | Everything, including school setup, concessions and changing published results |
| Admissions | The full student register and the admissions pipeline |
| Accounts | Fees, payments and receipts, with contact details for follow-up only |
| Teacher | Only the students in their own classes, with ID numbers masked and no address |

## Students and guardians

Guardian phone numbers and emails, home addresses, national ID numbers and medical notes are encrypted before they are saved; name, admission number and class stay searchable. Supported ID formats: B-Form/CNIC, Aadhaar (last 4 digits only), APAAR, Bangladesh birth registration/NID, Emirates ID and passport. Each student has a document checklist linked to files in Documents. A student's data can be exported, or erased on request while the fee records are kept under an anonymised name.

## Admissions, attendance and exams

- **Admissions**: from application to enrolment.
- **Attendance**: a daily register per class. Teachers can correct a register for 3 days; after that only the principal can.
- **Exams**: enter marks, then publish. Published results are locked; a change needs the principal and a reason, and the old mark is kept.

## Fees

Fee structures with fee heads in PKR, INR, BDT or AED. Invoices are generated for a period without ever billing a student twice. Concessions (sibling, merit, staff child, need-based, scholarship) need a second person to approve. Payments can't overpay an invoice, receipts are numbered in sequence, and mistakes are reversed with a reason, never deleted. Late fees, an overdue list and collection totals by payment method are included.

## Parent links

A read-only page for one child's fees, attendance and published results, valid for 30 days by default (90 at most). It never shows ID numbers, medical notes or the address, and it can be withdrawn at any time.

## Current limits

Fees are recorded, not collected online (no payment gateway yet). There are no SMS or WhatsApp fee reminders, timetables, transport or hostel modules, or mobile screens yet. No claim is made of compliance with India's DPDP Act, the UAE PDPL, or Pakistani or Bangladeshi data-protection law; the features help a school meet its own obligations.
