# Inaya Commerce: the Shop Counter

## Commerce: the Shop Counter (Front Office)

For: Cashiers, supervisors, shop managers.

Point of Sale is your till. It works on a normal computer, a laptop, a tablet or a phone. Every sale updates your stock, your books and the cash in the drawer automatically. Find it under Sales > Shop counter > Point of Sale.

### Start your day: open a shift

1. Open Point of Sale. At the top, choose your till (register).
2. Press "Open shift" and type the cash already in the drawer (the float).
3. You are ready to sell.

### Make a sale

1. Scan the barcode, type the product name, or tap the product.
2. To sell more than one, press + or type the number.
3. Press "Pay" (or F9). Choose Cash, Bank transfer or another method and type the amount. The till shows the change.
4. Press complete. The receipt appears: print it, email it or save it.

### Returns, discounts and approvals

1. Returns: open the Sales tab, find the sale (or type its receipt number), press Return and choose the items.
2. Discounts above your limit need a supervisor or manager to approve them from their own screen (Approvals tab). This stops mistakes and misuse.

### End your day: close the shift

1. Count the cash in the drawer.
2. Press "Close shift" and type the amount you counted.
3. Inaya shows if the drawer is over or short. Big differences are flagged for the manager.

> Keyboard shortcuts: F2 search, F4 customer, F8 park a sale for later, F9 pay, Esc close a window.
