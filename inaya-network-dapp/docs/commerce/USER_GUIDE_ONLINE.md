# Inaya Commerce: Online Selling, Shopify and WooCommerce

## Commerce: Online Selling, Shopify and WooCommerce

For: Shop owners, online sales staff.

Sell online from the same products, stock and books as your shop. You can have your own Inaya web store, and connect a Shopify or WooCommerce store you already have. Find it under Sales > Online store & Shopify / WooCommerce.

### Your own Inaya web store

1. Open the Store tab. Press "Create store" and choose your store's web address.
2. Add your logo, colours and home page text, then press "Save draft".
3. Open the Products tab and choose which products to show online.
4. Set up Delivery and Payments (bank transfer, cash on delivery, or cards through your own Stripe or Safepay account).
5. Press "Publish store". Online orders arrive under Orders and take stock from the same shelves.

### Connect WooCommerce

1. In WordPress, open WooCommerce > Settings > Advanced > REST API and create a key with Read/Write access.
2. In Inaya, open the "Shopify & WooCommerce" tab and press "Connect WooCommerce".
3. Type your shop address, then paste the Consumer key (ck_...) and Consumer secret (cs_...). Press "Save keys".
4. Press "Import & match products". Inaya matches your WooCommerce products to your Inaya products.
5. From now on, stock updates go to WooCommerce and new WooCommerce orders come into Inaya once, with their stock taken off.

### Connect Shopify

1. Open the "Shopify & WooCommerce" tab and press "Connect Shopify".
2. Type your Shopify address (yourshop.myshopify.com) and press "Install Inaya on Shopify", then approve on the Shopify screen.
3. Press "Import & match products", then use "Send stock now" and "Get orders now" whenever you want an immediate update.

> One-click Shopify installation is being reviewed by Shopify for all shops. If the install does not work for your shop yet, contact us and we will connect it with you.

> Your plan decides how many stores you can connect: Commerce Starter one (Shopify or WooCommerce), the Business add-on one of each, Enterprise three.
